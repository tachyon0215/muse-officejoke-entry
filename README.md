# muse-officejoke-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [The Funniest Office Joke, Judged Head to Head](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-funniest-office-joke-judged-head-to-head-3dc3233c).

## The entry

> Our office calls it 'the cloud' because nobody knows where the files are, it rains errors every Friday, and Finance just keeps paying for the weather.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A universal office truth built as an escalating triple - lost files, Friday errors, Finance paying regardless - with the surprise punchline word 'weather' held to the very end, and none of the brief's stock meeting/reply-all/printer material.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 c3280a90bd566c1fd8751d27c2681a8f7a938fb512ba1fd016e2db346df81ebc).
